# Goryeo architecture atlas

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-23. The first realistic draft was rejected; Architecture.png is the pixel-style revision. This is fantasy Goryeo-inspired art, not a historical reconstruction.

The original RGBA atlas is retained. `Tools/MapGen/goryeo_art.py` defines measured sprite rectangles, final cell dimensions, a 28-colour palette per sprite and binary alpha. Tiles are baked to actual 16px cells; there is no HD overlay on the pixel-art game.

Cohesive exquisitely crafted high-detail pixel-art-like hand painted sprites with crisp silhouettes. Row1 middle: modest Korean giwa courtyard merchant house, charcoal-blue curved tile roof, ivory plaster, rich walnut timber, open wooden veranda, warm paper windows. Row1 right: Korean market shop with tiled roof and faded indigo cloth awning, elegant celadon jars and rolled fabrics displayed along front, no signs. Row2 left: rustic thatched Korean cottage, golden straw roof with soft rounded ridge, warm plaster wall, drying herbs and clay jars. Row2 middle: open Korean timber pavilion on a stone base with blue-grey hipped roof, delicate jade beams and four wooden posts, no surrounding scenery. Row2 right: Korean windswept mature red pine with layered broad horizontal boughs, deep teal green needles, warm twisting trunk, natural irregular silhouette, not a Christmas tree. Soft light from upper left, subtle contact shadows under each object only. Quiet muted forest jade, celadon, blue slate, ochre and warm ivory palette. Premium indie game environmental asset quality, intricate believable roof tile rows and timber joinery, NOT generic orange Japanese ninja buildings, no torii, no pagoda tower, no photorealism, no outlines wider than fine dark edges. All six objects visually separate with no overlaps; no other objects in margins. Large but low and broad building forms.
